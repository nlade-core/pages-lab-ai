Real bug caught building this: the quantized vision encoder gives measurably wrong rankings on a real test photo (confirmed directly — this dog photo's top match flips from "dog" to "screenshot of computer code" when the vision tower is quantized). Ships fp32 vision (~43MB) + quantized text (~41MB), same fp32-for-vision lesson as the image classifier page, this time caught before shipping rather than after.
