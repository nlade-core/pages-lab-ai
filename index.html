pages-lab-ai

An actual trained neural network — MobileNetV2, 1000 ImageNet classes — running entirely in your browser. Upload a photo, it tells you what's in it. No server, nothing uploaded anywhere.

Running full fp32 weights, not quantized — this model's published int8 export is measurably broken (confidently mislabels obvious photos; verified before shipping this).

First experiment in the pages-lab / pages-pyodide family — JS-native this time (transformers.js), not Pyodide, since real model inference wants GPU/WASM acceleration Pyodide's CPU-only Python can't give it. Source.

Loads MobileNetV2 — about 14MB, cached by your browser after the first visit. Nothing downloads until you click.

Drop a photo here, or click to choose one.

100% client-side. No upload, no server, no account. Part of the pages-lab family of experiments.
