pages-lab-ai

A Gemini Nano chat that remembers more than one conversation. Saved to your browser's own localStorage — nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is loaded into the model just from opening this page. Click a saved conversation on the left and that's the moment it's actually replayed into a real model session; everything else just sits in your browser doing nothing until you ask for it.

Eleventh page in the pages-lab-ai family, alongside the image classifier, semantic search, chat, speech to text, text to speech, Chrome's built-in AI, code chat, a Pyodide notebook, thinking chat, and code agent. Companion to pages-lab's storage experiment. Chrome desktop only. Source.
